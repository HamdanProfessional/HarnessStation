Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: HarnessStation runs on your machine, needs no account, and sends us nothing about what you do with it.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Plain-language summary. There is no account and no telemetry. Your chats, files, settings and API keys live on your device. The only time any of your data leaves your machine is (1) when your chosen model provider processes your prompt — under their policy, with your key — or (2) if you deliberately turn on cloud sync, in which case it is encrypted on your device first and we only ever store unreadable ciphertext.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the HarnessStation desktop app, the browser version at hsapp.retris.io, and the small hosted gateway (hsapi.retris.io) that powers shared, non-personal features. It does not cover the third-party model providers or MCP servers you choose to connect — those have their own policies.

What stays on your device

  • Conversations, agents, workflows, skills and knowledge bases — stored as plain files under ~/.harnessx on desktop, or in your browser's local storage on the web.
  • API keys and vault secrets — held in your operating system keychain on desktop (browser local storage on the web), never transmitted to us.
  • Your prompts and the model's replies — never sent to us. They go only to the model provider you selected, using the key you provided.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-up or email is required to use the app.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of data — we don't have your data to sell.

The hosted gateway

A few features rely on a small shared server so they work without you hosting anything. It handles:

  • Public catalogs — the model benchmark feed and the MCP server directory. These are the same for everyone and contain none of your data.
  • The community library — when you choose to publish or import a skill, agent, workflow, template or schedule. Anything you publish is public by design; anything you import is your choice.
  • A CORS proxy for the browser build, so it can reach model providers that don't send the headers a browser needs. The proxy forwards your request to the provider you chose and streams the reply back. It is a relay: requests are not logged with your prompt contents, and your key is used only to complete that single request.
  • Optional cloud sync — see below.

Standard, short-lived web-server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested path) may exist transiently for security and abuse prevention, as with any server. They are not used to build a profile of you.

Cloud sync (opt-in, end-to-end encrypted)

Cloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on to back up or move your data between devices:

  • An account is identified by an email address and password. From your password, your device derives a verifier (used to sign in) and a separate encryption key that never leaves your device.
  • Your data is encrypted on your device with that key before it is uploaded. The server stores only the resulting ciphertext and cannot read it. This is zero-knowledge: we could not hand over your readable data even if compelled, because we never have it.
  • API keys and vault secrets are excluded from sync entirely — you re-enter them once per device.
  • Because only you hold the key, a forgotten password means the encrypted backup cannot be recovered — by you or by us. That is the intended trade-off for true end-to-end encryption.
  • You can sign out, stop syncing, or delete your account and its stored ciphertext at any time from the app.

Third-party model providers and MCP servers

When you send a prompt to a cloud model, or connect an MCP server or an external tool, your request is processed by that third party under their terms and privacy policy, using the credentials you supplied. Choose providers you trust; local models keep even that on your own hardware.

Children

HarnessStation is a general-purpose developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above. Material changes to how optional features like cloud sync work will be reflected here.
