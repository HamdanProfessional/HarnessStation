Starting a call

Your first spoken conversation.

What a call needs

Hearing + speaking

  • Hearing — Whisper, local speech-to-text
  • Speaking — a voice engine (next episode)

First-call download

Pick a Whisper size

tiny   fastest, roughest
base   the sensible default
small  slowest, most accurate

Three input modes

Auto · Push-to-talk · Wake word

  • Auto — continuous; replies when you pause (use headphones)
  • Push-to-talk — hold Ctrl+Shift+V, global, works from any app
  • Wake word — waits for your trigger phrase

After the call

Saved, and reopens as a call

A finished call lands in the sidebar and resumes as a call when you reopen it.

Gotcha

An empty call isn't saved

Start one with no exchange and it vanishes — a mis-press leaves no clutter.
