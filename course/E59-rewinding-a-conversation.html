Rewinding a conversation

Step the chat itself back to an earlier point.

The action

Pick a point, rewind to it

  • Everything after that point is removed from the chat
  • You're back at that moment, free to go another way

The safety net

Snapshot first, delete second

rewindTo(index)
   ↓
snapshot current state
   ↓
delete everything after index
   ← reversible

Rewind vs. Branch (E46)

Fork a new chat, or mutate this one

  • Branch from here — keeps the original, forks a new chat
  • Rewind — mutates this chat back to an earlier state

Choosing between them

One chat, or two to compare?

  • Rewind — a clean redo, no extra chat
  • Branch — keep both paths, compare them side by side
