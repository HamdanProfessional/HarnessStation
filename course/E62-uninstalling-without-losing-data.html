Uninstalling without losing your data

The app leaves, your data stays — until you say otherwise.

The uninstall step

Windows Settings, or dpkg -r

Windows   Settings → Apps → uninstall
Linux     dpkg -r <package>

What's left behind

~/.harnessx survives, on purpose

  • Conversations, config, and data stay in ~/.harnessx
  • Uninstall removes the program, not your history

Actually deleting it

Delete ~/.harnessx by hand

rm -rf ~/.harnessx   ← the real “delete everything”

If you're backing up instead

models/ and engines/ are worth skipping

  • models/, engines/ — large, but re-downloadable
  • Exclude them from backups to save real space
