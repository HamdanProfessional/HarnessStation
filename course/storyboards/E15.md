## Slide 3 · ~10s

**Narration:** On your very first call, the app downloads a Whisper model — a one-time thing. You pick the size: tiny is fastest and roughest, small is slowest and most accurate, and base sits in the middle. Base is a good default; start there.

**On screen / action:** APP: on first call, show the Whisper model download prompt; mention tiny/base/small.

## Slide 4 · ~13s

**Narration:** Three ways to talk to it. Auto — it listens continuously and replies when you stop; best with headphones. Push-to-talk — hold Control Shift V, which is global, so it works even from another app. And Wake word — it waits until you say the trigger. Pick Auto for a hands-free chat at your desk.

## Slide 5 · ~10s

**Narration:** When you end a call, it's saved just like a chat — you'll find it in the sidebar, and reopening it resumes as a call, not a transcript you have to read. Your spoken conversations are first-class, not throwaway.

**On screen / action:** APP: end the call; show it appears in the sidebar as a chat, and reopens as a call.

## Slide 6 · ~8s

**Narration:** One small gotcha, and it's a nice one: if you start a call and never actually say anything, it isn't saved. A mis-press doesn't leave empty clutter in your sidebar — only real exchanges stick around.
