## Slide 1 · ~6s

**Narration:** A quick reference for when the presets don't cover the cadence you need: cron expressions, in four worked examples.

## Slide 2 · ~10s

**Narration:** A cron expression is five fields — minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week — each either a number or a star meaning any. Presets cover the common cases; cron covers everything else.

## Slide 3 · ~9s

**Narration:** Weekday mornings at eight: zero, eight, star, star, one through five. The weekday field takes a range, so Monday through Friday is one dash five.

**On screen / action:** APP: enter 0 8 * * 1-5 as the schedule's cron expression.

## Slide 4 · ~9s

**Narration:** Every four hours: zero minutes past, and a slash-four on the hour field means every fourth hour starting from zero — midnight, four, eight, and on.

**On screen / action:** APP: enter 0 */4 * * * as the schedule's cron expression.

## Slide 5 · ~9s

**Narration:** Monthly, on the first at nine: zero, nine, one in the day-of-month field, star for month, star for weekday.

**On screen / action:** APP: enter 0 9 1 * * as the schedule's cron expression.

## Slide 6 · ~9s

**Narration:** Friday evening at five: zero, seventeen for the 24-hour clock, star, star, and five for Friday in the weekday field.

**On screen / action:** APP: enter 0 17 * * 5 as the schedule's cron expression.
