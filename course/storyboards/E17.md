## Slide 1 · ~6s

**Narration:** Let's make a call feel like an actual conversation, not a sequence of announcements. That means being able to interrupt it, and tuning how it delivers speech.

## Slide 2 · ~10s

**Narration:** Turn on barge-in. Now you can talk over the avatar to interrupt it — the moment you start speaking, it stops and listens, exactly like interrupting a person. No waiting for it to finish a paragraph you've already heard enough of.

**On screen / action:** SWITCH TO APP: Settings › Voice, enable barge-in; back in a call, talk over the avatar to cut it off.

## Slide 3 · ~12s

**Narration:** Here's the important gotcha, and you should demonstrate it. Barge-in through speakers makes the app hear its own voice and interrupt itself, over and over — it's unusable on speakers. Headphones fix it completely. If barge-in seems broken, this is almost always why.

**On screen / action:** IMPORTANT DEMO: on speakers, show it interrupting itself; then put on headphones and show it working.

## Slide 4 · ~13s

**Narration:** In Settings, Voice, a few controls change how it sounds. Human delivery adds natural pacing. Expressiveness goes zero, one, two — flat to lively. Persona shapes its manner. And Speech rewrite is clever: a small model rewrites each reply for the ear — numbers become words, markdown is stripped, sentences get shorter — so it reads aloud naturally.

**On screen / action:** APP: Settings › Voice — point at Human delivery, Expressiveness (0/1/2), Persona, Speech rewrite.

## Slide 5 · ~12s

**Narration:** One common complaint: it hears me but doesn't reply. Two controls fix that. The level meter shows whether it's actually picking up your voice. And silence timeout, plus smart endpointing, decide how long a pause means you're done — turn them up if it keeps cutting you off, down if it waits too long.

**On screen / action:** APP: show the level meter reacting to your voice; open the silence-timeout / smart-endpointing settings.
