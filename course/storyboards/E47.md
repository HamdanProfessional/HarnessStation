## Slide 2 · ~12s

**Narration:** Back to the loop from Episode 11: eight tool calls means nine requests, not one. And the ninth resends everything from the first eight — the whole growing history goes out again on every single lap. That resend is why agentic tasks cost so much more than a single question.

## Slide 3 · ~12s

**Narration:** Where the money actually goes: input tokens. Global, project, and chat instructions; recalled memory; retrieved knowledge passages; every enabled tool's definition; and the entire conversation history, resent each lap. The reply you get back is usually the cheap part.

## Slide 4 · ~13s

**Narration:** Ranked, what actually saves money: pick a smaller model for most work, compact long chats, start a fresh chat for a new topic instead of dragging the old one along, enable fewer tools, be specific so the loop stays short, and split large tasks into smaller ones.

## Slide 5 · ~10s

**Narration:** What does not help much: shortening your own message, turning memory off, or avoiding tools altogether. None of those touch the real sinks — instructions, history, and tool definitions dwarf a shorter question every time.

## Slide 6 · ~11s

**Narration:** Set caps in Settings, Usage. Once a cap is reached, the app stops rather than continuing silently — you get a clear halt, not a surprise bill.
