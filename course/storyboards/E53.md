# E53 · What gets shared, and what never does

*Estimated voiceover runtime: 1m 13s. Generated from the deck — edit the deck, then re-run build-subtitles.mjs.*

## Slide 1 · ~7s

**Narration:** Pairing two machines is easy. Knowing exactly what that pairing lets one do on the other is the part worth getting right — three switches, and a boundary that no switch can move.

## Slide 2 · ~13s

**Narration:** Three switches, all off by default. Models: let this device run inference using the remote's keys or GPU. Tools: let it call tools on the remote. Knowledge: let it search knowledge bases stored on the remote. Nothing is shared until you turn one on.

**On screen / action:** SWITCH TO APP: paired device's sharing panel — three toggles, all off by default.

## Slide 3 · ~15s

**Narration:** Here's the boundary that doesn't move. Even with Tools switched on, shell, Python, and file-writing tools are never shared across the mesh — full stop. That's not a setting you can find and enable; it's a hard line in the design, because those three are exactly the tools that can do lasting damage to a machine that isn't yours to risk.

## Slide 4 · ~10s

**Narration:** For any paired device, What can it do? spells out exactly which switches are on and what that grants — no guessing from the toggle state alone.

**On screen / action:** APP: open “What can it do?” on a paired device.

## Slide 5 · ~8s

**Narration:** Turn Models on and the remote's models simply appear in your model picker alongside your local and cloud ones — same picker, no separate workflow.

**On screen / action:** APP: open the model picker, point at the remote device's models listed alongside local ones.

## Slide 6 · ~14s

**Narration:** If something's not connecting: check mesh is on at both ends, not just one. Discovery is a network broadcast that guest or corporate Wi-Fi often blocks — add by address instead. The ports are 8793 for mesh traffic and 8794 for announcements, useful if you're touching a firewall. And a sleeping machine will correctly show offline — that's not a bug.
