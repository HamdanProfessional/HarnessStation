## Slide 3 · ~13s

**Narration:** Five things worth being careful with: terminal tool reach — how much of the filesystem it can touch. Browser session delegation — it can act inside your logged-in browser. Unattended schedules — running with nobody watching. The mesh's plaintext transport. And passive memory accumulation, quietly building up over time.

**Narration:** Last point: sharing a conversation export is safe in one specific sense and risky in another. It contains everything the model read — never your keys, those stay in the credential store. So the risk isn't credential leakage, it's your codebase's contents. Read before you share.
