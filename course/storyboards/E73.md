## Slide 3 · ~10s

**Narration:** The feature: let a viewer mute one kind of notification — say, info toasts, which fire the most and matter least — without touching success or error. Small, real, and it needs a setting that survives a restart, not just component state.

## Slide 4 · ~13s

**Narration:** That's why this hooks into the real store.ts, not toast.ts's local one — a mute preference belongs alongside every other durable setting, saved to settings.json like everything in E44. Add a field to the settings shape, mutedToastKinds, and use the store's existing update action to change it — the same pattern any settings panel in the app already uses.

**On screen / action:** SWITCH TO EDITOR: open src/lib/store.ts — find the settings shape and its update action, e.g. updateSettings().

## Slide 5 · ~13s

**Narration:** toast.ts's push function is where every notification is born, so that's where muting has to be enforced: read the current mutedToastKinds from the main store, and if this kind is in it, skip appending to history and skip incrementing unread. One conditional, right at the source.

**On screen / action:** SWITCH TO EDITOR: open src/lib/toast.ts — find push(), add a check against useStore.getState().settings.mutedToastKinds before incrementing history/unread.

## Slide 6 · ~12s

**Narration:** Last piece: a control inside the bell's own dropdown, next to Clear — a small toggle per kind, calling updateSettings straight from the component, exactly the way ChatWindow.tsx called store actions in E66. No new panel, no new route — the feature lives entirely inside the component it extends.

**On screen / action:** SWITCH TO EDITOR: in NotificationBell.tsx, add a small mute toggle per kind inside the bell-panel dropdown, calling updateSettings().
