## Slide 5 · ~13s

**Narration:** A little of the Rust underneath, for credibility. hw_info detects total RAM, AVX2 support, and an NVIDIA GPU via nvidia-smi — that is what powers the fit check. Downloads stream to a temporary .part file and only rename to the final name on success. And archives — zip and tar.gz — are extracted in-process, with path-traversal protection so a malicious archive can't write outside the models folder.

## Slide 6 · ~9s
