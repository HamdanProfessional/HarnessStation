## Slide 5 · ~13s

**Narration:** A word on enabling. Tools are chosen per conversation — you decide which are on in each chat. A new chat starts with the built-ins enabled so it can act immediately, but the habit that matters is trimming: turn off what a task doesn't need. Because — and this is a theme for the rest of the course — forty enabled tools choose worse than five. When you want a fixed, reusable set, that's an agent, in Module 10.
