## Slide 3 · ~12s

**Narration:** Most providers speak the same OpenAI-compatible format, so it's just a different base URL each time. Here are the common ones — OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Groq, Together. Groq and OpenRouter even have real free tiers, so you can try a cloud model at no cost.

## Slide 4 · ~12s

**Narration:** Here's the important part. When you save, your key is written to your operating system's credential store — not into settings.json on disk. And it's only ever sent to the one provider it belongs to. It never passes through us, and it never touches your transcripts.

**On screen / action:** APP: after saving, reopen Settings to show the key field masked / not shown in plain text. Optionally show it's absent from settings.json.
