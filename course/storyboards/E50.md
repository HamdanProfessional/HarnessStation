# E50 · Loading and running a local model

## Slide 1 · ~7s

**Narration:** Track C starts here — local models running on your own hardware, under the app's supervision. This episode: load a downloaded model, tune the basics, and use it from chat exactly like a cloud provider.

## Slide 2 · ~9s

**Narration:** Once a model is downloaded it shows up in My Models. From there it's one button: Load. The app spawns a supervised llama-server for it in the background, and Eject shuts that server down cleanly when you're done.

**On screen / action:** SWITCH TO APP: Settings › My Models. Select a downloaded model.

## Slide 3 · ~12s

**Narration:** Two controls matter before you load. Context length sets how much conversation the model can hold at once — bigger costs more memory. GPU offload layers decides how many of the model's layers run on your GPU versus the CPU; more offloaded layers usually means faster generation, up to what your VRAM can hold.

**On screen / action:** APP: show the context-length field and the GPU-offload-layers slider before loading.

## Slide 4 · ~10s

**Narration:** As you adjust those two controls, an estimated memory readout updates live — so you can see you're about to overshoot your VRAM before you hit Load, not after it fails.

**On screen / action:** APP: point at the estimated memory readout as the offload slider moves.

## Slide 5 · ~12s

**Narration:** Here's the payoff. Open the chat's provider picker and there's now a Local entry, right alongside your cloud providers. Pick it, and you're talking to the model you just loaded — through the exact same OpenAI-compatible client the app uses for every cloud provider.

**On screen / action:** SWITCH TO APP: Chat provider picker — show the new “Local” entry and send a message.

## Slide 6 · ~10s

**Narration:** That's the whole point of the design: local isn't a special code path bolted on the side. It's just another endpoint behind the same interface every other provider uses, which is why switching between local and cloud mid-project is completely seamless.
