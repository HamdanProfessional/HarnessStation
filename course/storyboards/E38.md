**Narration:** A real, model-visible browser window, driven from inside a conversation. Let's turn it on and watch it open and read a page.

## Slide 2 · ~10s

**Narration:** Turn on the Browser tool group. Once it's on, ask the agent to open a page and summarize something on it — that's the whole loop for this episode.

**On screen / action:** SWITCH TO APP: enable the Browser tool group in this chat's tools.

## Slide 3 · ~12s

**Narration:** The tool list: open_url loads a page, read_all_text and find_text pull content off it, list_buttons and click_button let it interact, and take_screenshot or read_screenshot let it look. That's enough to browse, search, and click through most pages.

**On screen / action:** APP: run open_url, then read_all_text or find_text; show list_buttons and click_button.

## Slide 4 · ~13s

**Narration:** Why a real positioned window, not an iframe embedded in the app? Cross-origin iframes can't be scripted from outside, and most real sites refuse to be framed at all. So the app opens an actual browser window and positions it — which is also why that panel holds still below the chat instead of scrolling along with it.

## Slide 5 · ~11s

**Narration:** One view, no tabs — this browser follows one page at a time, the way you'd expect from something the model is reading directly rather than juggling.

## Slide 6 · ~11s

**Narration:** The gotcha: the in-app browser cannot take a screenshot — that capability needs the browser extension, which is next episode. Combined with having only one view and no tabs, it's built for reading and clicking, not for visual work.

## Slide 7 · ~6s

**Narration:** For a session you're already signed into, or for real screenshots and tabs, the browser extension is the next step.
