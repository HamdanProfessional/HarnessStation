## Slide 4 · ~12s

**Narration:** What matters most here: updates are signed. Before anything is applied, the app verifies that signature. If the check fails — a corrupted download, a tampered file, anything that doesn't match — the update is refused outright, not silently applied. Fail closed, not open.

## Slide 5 · ~11s

**Narration:** Why bother signing at all? Because an update replaces the app's own binary — the one thing on your machine that's implicitly trusted. A silent bad update would be the worst possible failure mode. Signing turns “trust the download” into “trust the download only if it proves who it's from.”
