## Slide 1 · ~7s

**Narration:** Uninstalling shouldn't mean losing every conversation and setting you've built up. Here's the clean uninstall path, and exactly where the “actually delete everything” line is.

## Slide 2 · ~10s

**Narration:** Uninstall the normal way for your platform: through Windows Settings, or dpkg minus r on Linux. Standard uninstall, nothing special to remember on this step.

## Slide 3 · ~12s

**Narration:** Here's the deliberate part: the ~/.harnessx folder is left behind. That's on purpose — it's your conversations, your keys, your configuration. An uninstall removes the program, not your history. If you reinstall later, it's all still there waiting.

## Slide 4 · ~10s

**Narration:** Want everything gone? Delete ~/.harnessx by hand. That's the actual line — the installer never touches it, so removing it is entirely your call.

**On screen / action:** TERMINAL: rm -rf ~/.harnessx (or delete the folder in File Explorer).

## Slide 5 · ~12s

**Narration:** If you're backing that folder up instead, know what's in it: models and engines are the large folders, and they're entirely re-downloadable. Excluding them from a backup saves gigabytes without losing anything that can't be fetched again.
