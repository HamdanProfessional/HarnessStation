Track A · Beginner · Episode 13

Getting better results

Prompt patterns that actually move the needle.

The high-value three

Constrain, permit, demand evidence

  • Say what you don't want — “only bugs, skip style”
  • Permit it to find nothing — kills padded output
  • Ask it to quote the source — beats confident invention

How you drive it

Plan, step, and handle “stuck”

  • Ask for a plan before multi-file work
  • Small steps beat one long ask
  • Say what to do when stuck — ask, or assume-and-flag

What doesn't help

Stop spending words here

  • Politeness, and “you are a senior engineer” framing
  • Very long instructions
  • Repeating an instruction it already ignored

Gotcha

Repeating ≠ fixing

If it ignored something, another layer is probably contradicting it. Find the conflict (Episode 11), don't just say it again, louder.
