# Slide 1
Connecting a media-generation engine — images, audio, video, even 3D — and generating something live, including a free local route.

# Slide 2
Settings, Media models, Add model. Four kinds: an OpenAI-compatible image endpoint, Stable Diffusion webui — free and local, but it needs the dash-dash-api flag — an OpenAI-compatible speech endpoint, and Replicate, which is the widest: image, audio, video, and 3D all through one connection.

# Slide 3
Turn on the Media tool group and ask for an image on camera. Two prompting habits worth showing: say what you don't want, not just what you want, and name the aspect ratio explicitly — or let the model write its own detailed prompt from a rough idea.

# Slide 4
For free local images with a GPU: run A1111 or Forge with the dash-dash-api flag, then point the Stable Diffusion webui model type at it. About six gigabytes of VRAM covers SDXL — no per-image cost, no external account.

# Slide 5
Speech works the same shape — an OpenAI-compatible speech endpoint, added the same way, generating audio from text on request.

# Slide 6
The gotcha: video and 3D through Replicate are considerably slower and dearer than images — sometimes minutes, sometimes real cost per generation. Say so out loud before anyone leaves an agent generating video unattended, so nobody discovers the bill after the fact.

# Slide 7
With models everywhere in this app, the natural next question is which one to actually use for a given job. Compare answers that in under a minute.
