# Slide 1
A quick reference for when the presets don't cover the cadence you need: cron expressions, in four worked examples.

# Slide 2
A cron expression is five fields — minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week — each either a number or a star meaning any. Presets cover the common cases; cron covers everything else.

# Slide 3
Weekday mornings at eight: zero, eight, star, star, one through five. The weekday field takes a range, so Monday through Friday is one dash five.

# Slide 4
Every four hours: zero minutes past, and a slash-four on the hour field means every fourth hour starting from zero — midnight, four, eight, and on.

# Slide 5
Monthly, on the first at nine: zero, nine, one in the day-of-month field, star for month, star for weekday.

# Slide 6
Friday evening at five: zero, seventeen for the 24-hour clock, star, star, and five for Friday in the weekday field.
