# Slide 1
Welcome to Track B. We start with voice — actually talking to your agent out loud. In this one we hold a first spoken conversation, end to end.

# Slide 2
Press New call at the top of the sidebar. A call needs two things: hearing — that's Whisper, running locally to turn your speech into text — and speaking, an engine that voices the reply, which is the next episode. Right now we focus on hearing.

# Slide 3
On your very first call, the app downloads a Whisper model — a one-time thing. You pick the size: tiny is fastest and roughest, small is slowest and most accurate, and base sits in the middle. Base is a good default; start there.

# Slide 4
Three ways to talk to it. Auto — it listens continuously and replies when you stop; best with headphones. Push-to-talk — hold Control Shift V, which is global, so it works even from another app. And Wake word — it waits until you say the trigger. Pick Auto for a hands-free chat at your desk.

# Slide 5
When you end a call, it's saved just like a chat — you'll find it in the sidebar, and reopening it resumes as a call, not a transcript you have to read. Your spoken conversations are first-class, not throwaway.

# Slide 6
One small gotcha, and it's a nice one: if you start a call and never actually say anything, it isn't saved. A mis-press doesn't leave empty clutter in your sidebar — only real exchanges stick around.

# Slide 7
It can hear you now. Next we give it a voice — comparing the four synthesis engines and landing on a sensible default.
