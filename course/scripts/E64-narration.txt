# Slide 1
This closes out the advanced track — a single reference episode that pulls every privacy and security point made across the whole series into one checklist, before you show this to anyone else.

# Slide 2
Start with what leaves the machine at all — restated from episode twelve as one table. Local models and local embeddings never leave. A cloud model call sends your prompt and context to that provider. Nothing else phones home on its own.

# Slide 3
Five things worth being careful with: terminal tool reach — how much of the filesystem it can touch. Browser session delegation — it can act inside your logged-in browser. Unattended schedules — running with nobody watching. The mesh's plaintext transport. And passive memory accumulation, quietly building up over time.

# Slide 4
Let's build private-by-default, live: a local model instead of cloud, local embeddings, Kokoro or Piper for speech instead of a cloud voice, Whisper for transcription, and the mesh either off entirely or restricted to LAN-only. Five switches, and nothing needs to leave the machine.

# Slide 5
After the fact, you can review exactly what an agent did: read the tool-call cards right in the chat, or grep the conversations folder under home dot harnessx for a tool name or a keyword. Nothing about an agent's actions is hidden from you.

# Slide 6
Last point: sharing a conversation export is safe in one specific sense and risky in another. It contains everything the model read — never your keys, those stay in the credential store. So the risk isn't credential leakage, it's your codebase's contents. Read before you share.
