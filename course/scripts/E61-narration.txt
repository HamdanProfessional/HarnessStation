# Slide 1
Module 16 is about deployment and staying current. First: how the app updates itself, and how it proves an update is trustworthy before applying it.

# Slide 2
The app checks for updates on startup, automatically, no action needed. If one's found you'll see it — otherwise it stays out of your way.

# Slide 3
Want to check right now instead of waiting for the next launch? Settings, Data and updates, Check for updates. Same check, on demand.

# Slide 4
What matters most here: updates are signed. Before anything is applied, the app verifies that signature. If the check fails — a corrupted download, a tampered file, anything that doesn't match — the update is refused outright, not silently applied. Fail closed, not open.

# Slide 5
Why bother signing at all? Because an update replaces the app's own binary — the one thing on your machine that's implicitly trusted. A silent bad update would be the worst possible failure mode. Signing turns “trust the download” into “trust the download only if it proves who it's from.”
