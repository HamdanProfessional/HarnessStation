# Slide 1
Now the other way to bring a model: a cloud provider with your own API key. It's a couple of fields — and I want to be very clear about exactly where that key ends up.

# Slide 2
Open Settings, Providers, and click Add provider. There are three fields: a name you choose, the base URL of the service, and your API key. Let's fill them in for OpenAI.

# Slide 3
Most providers speak the same OpenAI-compatible format, so it's just a different base URL each time. Here are the common ones — OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Groq, Together. Groq and OpenRouter even have real free tiers, so you can try a cloud model at no cost.

# Slide 4
Here's the important part. When you save, your key is written to your operating system's credential store — not into settings.json on disk. And it's only ever sent to the one provider it belongs to. It never passes through us, and it never touches your transcripts.

# Slide 5
One small thing you might notice. Anthropic is listed on its own because it's a native integration — it uses Anthropic's messages API rather than the OpenAI-compatible format. You don't need to do anything different; just pick Anthropic as the kind and it's handled.
