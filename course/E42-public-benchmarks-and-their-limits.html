Why this one is different

The limitation

Contaminated by the training data itself

  • Benchmark questions are widely public — models may have seen them in training
  • A top score can mean memorization as much as skill

A weak proxy for your task

Shortlist with it — do not decide with it

  • Good for narrowing a long model list to a shortlist
  • Bad for predicting how it performs on your specific job

Gotcha
